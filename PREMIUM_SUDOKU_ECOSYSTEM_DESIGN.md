# Premium Sudoku Ecosystem - Economy Lock

## Philosophy
Sweirki Sudoku uses a premium-friendly economy: gameplay is never interrupted, competitive power is never sold, and monetization comes from convenience, cosmetics, season value, and optional rewarded ads.

## Currency Model

### Coins
App-wide soft currency. Coins connect Classic, Daily, Weekly, premium variants, Arena, goals, and rewarded ads.

Earned from:
- Classic completions
- Daily/Weekly challenges
- Killer/Hyper/X Sudoku clears
- Arena wins
- Season rewards
- Rewarded Ad Center
- Sweirki Plus daily bonus

Spent on:
- extra Survival entries after free daily runs
- future power recharges outside gameplay
- future cosmetics or profile polish

### Tickets
Special Arena entry currency.

Earned from:
- starter wallet
- Weekly challenge
- Tournament champion rewards
- Sweirki Plus daily bonus
- Season Pass rewards
- Ticket fragments from Reward Center
- Ticket packs / bundles

Spent on:
- Tournament Cup after the daily free cup
- future special Arena events

### Ticket Fragments
Rewarded-ad bridge to Tickets. Ten fragments automatically convert to one Ticket.

### Arena Points
Competitive-only prestige currency from Arena performance. Arena Points are not sold and not granted by ads. They are used in the Arena Shop for prestige cosmetics.

### Season XP
Progression track XP earned across the app. It unlocks Season Pass reward levels.

## Mode Access Rules

- Ranked Arena: free, always.
- Power Arena: free entry, no premium gate.
- Survival Arena: one free daily run; Plus gets two. Extra runs cost Coins.
- Tournament: first daily cup free; later cups cost Tickets.
- Premium boards remain tied to Sweirki Plus.

## Ad Policy
Only rewarded ads are allowed.

Never show:
- interstitial ads
- banner ads
- ads during any Sudoku board
- ads after a win/loss automatically
- forced ads

Rewarded ads live only in Reward Center or explicit optional claim buttons.

## Monetization Products

1. Sweirki Plus Lifetime
- non-consumable / entitlement
- no ads
- premium boards
- daily ticket + coins
- premium cosmetics
- premium season value

2. Logic Wars Season Pass
- season product / entitlement or non-consumable per season
- premium track rewards
- cosmetics, tickets, coins

3. 10 Arena Tickets
- consumable
- tournament/event entry

4. Champion Bundle
- consumable bundle
- tickets, coins, cosmetics

## Competitive Integrity
Never sell:
- wins
- rating
- Arena Points
- league promotion
- easier puzzles
- tournament victories
